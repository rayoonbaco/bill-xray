# Power Mapper — Pass 7 contract

Identify only explicit statutory mechanics that create, assign, condition, limit, delegate, or structure governmental authority.

Always preserve:
- the verified citation anchor;
- the government actor when explicit;
- obligation vs discretion (`shall/must` vs `may`);
- prohibitions and limits;
- rulemaking, enforcement, waiver, oversight, structural, and federal/state mechanics;
- cross-reference uncertainty.

Do not call a provision constitutional, unconstitutional, authoritarian, deregulatory, expansive, narrow, good, bad, or politically beneficial. Those are later analytical judgments.

When the actor or scope is unclear, return `needs_legal_context` rather than a fluent guess.
